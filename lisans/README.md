## 📂 Structure by Year

- **`2021/`**:
  - `Devre_Analizi/`: Fundamental resistive circuit networks, Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Current and Voltage Laws (KCL / KVL), and nodal voltage matrix methods.
- **`2022/`**:
  - `Devre_Analizi/`: First-order RC step response, natural and forced response, and $\tau = RC$ time constant oscilloscope measurement.
  - `Sayisal_Sistemler_ve_Verilog/`: Complete 9-stage digital electronics series: TTL gate voltage transfer curves, decoders, 74LS151 MUX, 74LS83 arithmetic adders, synchronous JK/D flip-flop counters, Xilinx ISE Spartan FPGA synthesis, and Verilog HDL Mealy Finite State Machine (FSM) modeling with testbench verification.
  - `Analog_Elektronik/`: Semiconductor PN junction diode $I-V$ forward/reverse characterization, BJT DC active-region biasing ($Q$-point), Common-Emitter AC small-signal gain amplification, and N-channel JFET saturation region $A_v \approx 20$ voltage amplifier.
- **`2023/`**:
  - `Kontrol_Sistemleri/`: Complete 7-stage classical control engineering practicum: MATLAB Control System Toolbox transfer functions, continuous-time State-Space ($A, B, C, D$) matrices, Simulink algebraic block diagram reduction, closed-loop PID parameter tuning, Bode frequency magnitude/phase stability margins, s-plane Root Locus poles, and 2nd-order underdamped step transient specifications ($M_p, t_s, t_r$).
  - `PLC_Endustriyel_Otomasyon/`: Siemens TIA Portal V17 automation series (Labs 1–6): Ladder logic inputs/outputs, contact seal-in circuits, internal M-merker flags, Set/Reset latches, TON/TOF/TP timers, CTU/CTD counters, comparison operators, and sequential conveyor belt process control (SFC).
- **`2024/`**:
  - `PLC_Endustriyel_Otomasyon/`: Labs 7–8: 4–20 mA / 0–10 V analog input signal calibration (`NORM_X` / `SCALE_X`) and comprehensive multi-motor automated manufacturing line program with safety E-stop interlocking.
  - `Haberlesme_Sistemleri/`: GNU Octave digital communication channel modeling, Fourier frequency transform, bandpass filtering, and signal reconstruction.
  - `Yari_Iletken_Uretim/`: Semiconductor fabrication assignment: Deal-Grove 700 nm thermal $SiO_2$ oxidation modeling and 250 nm reactive ion etching (RIE) trench process calculations.
  - `Radar_Sistemleri/`: Coherent Pulse Doppler radar architecture, radar range equation, PRF blind speed avoidance, and target Doppler shift velocity estimation.
  - `Guc_Elektronigi/`: Single-phase phase-controlled half-wave thyristor (SCR) rectifier circuit with $R=100\,\Omega, L=50\,\text{mH}$ inductive load, freewheeling diode conduction, and dual-channel oscilloscope transient analysis.
