## 🔬 Practicum Modules & Experimental Topics

### 1. Exp 1: Temel Dirençli Devreler ve Ohm Kanunu (Fundamental Resistive Networks & Ohm's Law)
* **Topics:** Linear resistor I-V characteristics, equivalent resistance ($R_{eq}$) reduction for series, parallel, and bridge networks, loading effect of measurement instruments, and theoretical vs. benchtop experimental verification.
* **Instrumentation:** Precision digital multimeter (DMM), benchtop regulated DC power supply, resistor decade box.

### 2. Exp 2: Kirchhoff Kanunları ve Düğüm Gerilimi Analizi (Kirchhoff's Laws & Nodal Voltage Analysis)
* **Topics:** Experimental validation of Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) at multi-branch nodes, Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) around independent planar loops, systematic nodal analysis matrix representation, and loop-current methods under variable DC source excitations.
* **Instrumentation:** Dual-channel DC power supply, precision ammeter, high-impedance digital voltmeter.
